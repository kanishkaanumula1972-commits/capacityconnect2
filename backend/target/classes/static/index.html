Why Capacity Connect?

Beyond a conventional Learning Management System

01

Competency Intelligence

Combines profile, skills, certificates, and assessment history into one dynamic competency picture.

02

Skill Gap Detection

Automatically compares current competency against target roles and surfaces the biggest gaps.

03

Intelligent Trainer Matching

Matches trainees with the most suitable trainer based on expertise, experience, and ratings.

04

Personalized Learning

Recommends courses ranked by how directly they close each individual's detected skill gaps.

05

Organizational Analytics

Gives leadership a real-time heatmap of skill health and gaps across the entire organization.

06

Closed-Loop Learning

Identify gap, learn, assess, measure improvement, and update competency automatically.
